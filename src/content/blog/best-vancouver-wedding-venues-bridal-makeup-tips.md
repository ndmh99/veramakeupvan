Choosing your dream wedding location is one of the biggest decisions you'll make during wedding planning. In Greater Vancouver, couples are spoiled for choice—from historic Edwardian mansions nestled in nature to ultra-sleek downtown ballrooms and waterfront terraces.

However, every venue comes with its own unique environment: lighting conditions, exposure to ocean humidity, wind, or flash photography. Your bridal look should be tailored to match your setting seamlessly.

Here is our insider guide to the **Top 10 Vancouver Wedding Venues**, along with specialized **bridal makeup tips** for each location to ensure you look radiant and photo-ready all day long.

## 1. Hycroft Manor (Shaughnessy)

![Hycroft Manor wedding venue in Vancouver](/blog/images/venue-1.png)

A grand Edwardian mansion boasting historic European columns, charming terraces, and manicured gardens. It is the epitome of classic elegance.

* **Environment:** Mix of soft shaded gardens and rich wooden indoor interiors.
* **Bridal Makeup Tip:** Opt for **Timeless Soft Glam**. Rich interior wood paneling can absorb light, while outdoor shaded gardens flatter warm tones. Ask for softly defined brows, warm peach/rose blushes, and a satin-finish foundation that radiates romance without looking greasy.

## 2. Stanley Park Pavilion & Formal Gardens

![Stanley Park Pavilion wedding venue in Vancouver](/blog/images/venue-2.png)

Located in the heart of world-famous Stanley Park, this heritage venue offers lush greenery, forest backdrops, and romantic outdoor lawn ceremonies.

* **Environment:** Shaded outdoor natural light and forest moisture.
* **Bridal Makeup Tip:** **Prioritize Waterproofing & Anti-Humidity Prep**. Outdoor park settings mean natural humidity from nearby ocean air and surrounding trees. Use a silicone-based, humidity-resistant primer and long-wear cream blushes topped with powder to prevent makeup from melting in the sun.

## 3. Brock House Restaurant (Jericho Beach)

![Brock House Restaurant wedding venue in Vancouver](/blog/images/venue-3.png)

Situated right on the edge of Jericho Beach, Brock House combines beachside ocean views, sweeping lawns, and historic charm.

* **Environment:** Bright outdoor sunlight, direct ocean breeze, and beach dampness.
* **Bridal Makeup Tip:** **Focus on Hair Security & Matte-Glow Balance**. Waterfront winds mean loose facial hairs will catch on sticky lip glosses! Choose a long-wear liquid lipstick or satin lip stain over gloss. Keep skin hydrated with oil-free moisturisers to counter wind drying while using a light dusting of translucent powder on the T-zone.

## 4. Fairmont Pacific Rim (Downtown Waterfront)

![Fairmont Pacific Rim wedding venue in Vancouver](/blog/images/venue-4.png)

An ultra-luxurious, modern hotel featuring soaring glass windows, harbour views, and opulent ballroom spaces.

* **Environment:** High flash photography, heavy indoor studio lighting, and crisp natural window light.
* **Bridal Makeup Tip:** **Precision Contouring & Flash-Safe Setting**. HD camera flashes in luxury ballrooms can wash out subtle makeup. Incorporate subtle cream contouring to add dimensional structure to your cheekbones. **Crucial:** Ensure your makeup artist uses silica-free translucent setting powder to prevent white "flashback" in flash photography.

## 5. Cecil Green Park House (UBC Campus)

![Cecil Green Park House wedding venue in Vancouver](/blog/images/venue-5.png)

A stunning heritage house perched on the cliffs of Point Grey overlooking the Georgia Strait, surrounded by private gardens.

* **Environment:** High-exposure outdoor sunlight transitioning to warm indoor reception lighting.
* **Bridal Makeup Tip:** **SPF Caution & Luminous Skin**. Daytime garden ceremonies here catch intense daylight. Avoid mineral sunscreens containing zinc oxide or titanium dioxide under your makeup, as they bounce flash photography back into the camera lens. Rely on chemical SPF or foundation with built-in light coverage instead.

## 6. Brix & Mortar (Yaletown)

![Brix and Mortar wedding venue in Vancouver](/blog/images/venue-6.png)

An iconic Yaletown venue famous for its glass-covered outdoor courtyard, exposed brick walls, trailing ivy, and intimate chandelier-lit dining rooms.

* **Environment:** Moody, warm brick backdrop with low indoor ambient lighting.
* **Bridal Makeup Tip:** **Rich Tones & Lash Definition**. Exposed brick and warm chandeliers can cast orange or dark tones on skin if makeup is too pale. Embrace a slightly deeper lip accent (like berry, mauve, or deep rose) and textured individual false lashes to make your eyes pop against moody brickwork.

## 7. The Polygon Gallery (North Vancouver)

![The Polygon Gallery wedding venue in Vancouver](/blog/images/venue-7.png)

A striking modern architectural venue situated at Lonsdale Quay with floor-to-ceiling glass windows and unmatched views of the Vancouver city skyline.

* **Environment:** Abundant indirect cool daylight and minimalist, clean aesthetic.
* **Bridal Makeup Tip:** **Sleek Modern Minimalist Aesthetic**. Ultra-bright, minimalist spaces highlight skin texture. Focus on flawless skin prep with hydrating hyaluronic acid serums and lightweight airbrush foundation. Keep eye makeup clean with defined winged liner and fresh, dewy cheeks.

## 8. Sunway / Dr. Sun Yat-Sen Classical Chinese Garden (Chinatown)

![Dr. Sun Yat-Sen Classical Chinese Garden wedding venue in Vancouver](/blog/images/venue-8.png)

North America's first traditional Chinese garden, featuring covered walkways, tranquil jade-green ponds, and intricate stone architecture.

* **Environment:** Enclosed covered stone areas, jade reflections, and shaded walkways.
* **Bridal Makeup Tip:** **Neutral Color Palette to Counter Green Casts**. Ponds and lush foliage can reflect subtle greenish undertones onto your skin in photos. Ask your bridal artist to use warm, rosy undertones in your concealer and setting powder to keep your complexion looking healthy, bright, and vibrant.

## 9. Swaneset Bay Resort & Country Club (Pitt Meadows)

![Swaneset Bay Resort wedding venue in Vancouver](/blog/images/venue-9.png)

A breathtaking 65,000-square-foot chateau clubhouse surrounded by sweeping golf greens and dramatic mountain ranges. Popular for both Western and large multi-day cultural celebrations.

* **Environment:** Expansive outdoor fields, direct sunlight, and long event durations.
* **Bridal Makeup Tip:** **Ultra Long-Wear Setting**. Weddings here often involve full-day itineraries and extensive outdoor photo sessions. Layering cream products under powder counterparts (cream blush under powder blush, eyeliner sealed with dark shadow) creates a bulletproof base that survives heat, movement, and tears of joy.

## 10. Rosewood Hotel Georgia (Downtown)

![Rosewood Hotel Georgia wedding venue in Vancouver](/blog/images/venue-10.png)

A landmark venue that exudes 1920s Roaring Twenties glamour, featuring dark wood paneling, crystal chandeliers, and vintage art deco architecture.

* **Environment:** Vintage indoor luxury lighting with warm gold hues.
* **Bridal Makeup Tip:** **Classic Vintage Hollywood Glamour**. Complement this historic space with a smooth matte or semi-matte base, a classic red or bold berry lip, and soft smokey eyeshadow with defined liquid liner.
