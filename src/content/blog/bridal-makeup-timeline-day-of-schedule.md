## 1. Golden Rules for Planning Your Bridal Makeup Timeline

Before diving into the hourly breakdown, keep these industry-proven scheduling rules in mind:

* **Calculate Time Per Service:** Allow **30 to 45 minutes** per service (hair OR makeup) for bridesmaids and mothers, and **60 to 90 minutes** per service for the bride.
* **Build in Buffer Time (Crucial!):** Always add a **30-minute buffer** right before the bride puts on her dress. Unexpected delays—like finding missing shoes or last-minute steam touches—happen to almost everyone.
* **Factor in Photographer Arrival:** Your photographer usually needs 30–45 minutes before you step into your dress to shoot detail flat-lays (invitations, rings, dress hanging) and catching final touch-up moments.

## 3. Where Should the Bride Go in the Timeline Sequence?

A very common question brides ask **Vera Makeup Van** during consultations is: "Should I get my makeup done first or last?"

> **The Best Order:** The bride should go **second-to-last or last** for makeup!

Getting your makeup done near the end ensures your look is fresh-faced for first looks and photos. However, never make yourself the very last slot without a buffer—if early morning services run 15 minutes over, you don't want your precious bridal prep time squeezed!

## 4. Tips to Keep Your Morning Prep Running On Time

1. **Clean & Dry Hair:** Remind all bridesmaids and mothers to wash their hair the night before (not the morning of) so it is 100% dry and ready to hold curls.
2. **Clean, Hydrated Skin:** Instruct everyone to arrive with a clean, freshly moisturized face and no remaining eye makeup from the night before.
3. **Wear Button-Down Shirts or Robes:** Ensure everyone wears tops that slide off easily without pulling over newly styled hair and makeup.
4. **Designate a "Prep Captain":** Assign your Maid of Honor or an organized friend to manage room food, playlists, and keeping people ready for their chair times.
