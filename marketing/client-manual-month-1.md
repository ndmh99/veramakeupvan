**Golden rules:**
- Reply to DMs within 2 hours — Instagram rewards speed.
- Never post without replying to comments from the last post.
- Post at the time in the calendar (Vancouver time). If you miss it, post anyway — don't skip the day.
- Reels are always **vertical (9:16), 15–60 seconds, filmed in natural light, first 2 seconds must show the before/after hook**. No watermark from other apps.

## Part 3 · Weekly content shoot (2 hours, Sunday or Monday)

Batch everything. One session, one week of content. What to capture each week:

**Always:**
- 2 client before/after transformations (front camera, same angle, same light) — if no client that week, do your own look
- 1 "how-to" clip (30 sec: setting spray demo, lash application, skin prep)
- 1 talking-to-camera clip (20 sec, answering one bridal question)
- 5–8 beauty close-up photos (eyes, skin, lips, finished look)
- 3 story-worthy clips (kit setup, product picks, studio BTS)

**Save originals** in a phone album called "CONTENT — Month 1". Don't upload screenshots of your camera roll.
